## What the extension accesses

When you activate Block mode on an X tab, X Quick Block reads page elements
needed to identify post authors and operate X's blocking controls. This
includes profile links and account handles, menu and confirmation-dialog
text, and the visible result of a block action. It checks the current tab's
URL to determine whether it is an eligible X page.

The extension does not request your password, read authentication cookies,
collect direct messages, or maintain a browsing-history database. It has no
analytics, advertising, or developer-operated backend.

## How information is used

Page information is processed locally to add Block buttons, identify the
chosen account, check the account named in X's confirmation dialog, and show
the result. Clicking an extension Block button authorizes it to operate X's
menu and confirm that block without a second click from you.

The extension does not send page information to the publisher. It operates
X's interface in your signed-in session; X receives and processes the block
action just as it does when you use its native controls. X's handling of
your account and block list is governed by X's own policies.

## Storage and retention

Block-mode state, processing status, and handles marked as blocked are kept
temporarily in memory for that page. The extension does not write these to
local storage, synchronized storage, or a developer server. Reloading the
page or closing the tab clears this temporary state. Turning Block mode off
removes the extension's controls but does not immediately clear every
in-memory value.

Blocks already submitted to X remain in your X account. Disabling or
uninstalling the extension does not undo them; manage them through X.

## Sharing and sale

The publisher does not receive, sell, or share the page data processed by
this extension. There are no third-party analytics or advertising services
in the extension. The user-requested interaction with X is described above.

## Your choices

Click the extension's toolbar icon to turn Block mode off, refresh or close
the tab to clear temporary state, or uninstall the extension through
Chrome's extension settings. Each block requires you to click a Block
button; there is no bulk or unattended blocking.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the extension's data practices change.
For privacy questions, use the publisher contact listed above. If you
contact the publisher, information you choose to send is handled by that
contact service and the publisher, not automatically collected by the extension.
